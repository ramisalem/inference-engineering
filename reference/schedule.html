A typical week

Three sessions of about 90 minutes beat one long Sunday, because spacing builds long-term memory.Spacing and retrieval practice are among the best-supported learning techniques: see Dunlosky et al., "Improving Students' Learning With Effective Learning Techniques" (2013).

  • Session 1: lesson A (concept, prediction, lab, exercises).
  • Session 2: lesson B.
  • Session 3: lesson C, or the spaced review: re-do the exercises you missed earlier, read the lesson's primary source, and update portfolio/LOG.md.

Behind schedule? Drop the stretch exercises in light lessons first. Keep the reviews and the checkpoints: they protect everything else you've learned.
